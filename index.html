Dead Acre

Midnight. A storm is rolling over the hills. You wake in your bedroom on the second floor and something is moving downstairs. Hold the house, from the basement to the attic, for as many waves as you can. Keep your flashlight on. Headshots hit four times harder, the blue ones run and pounce, each wave hides a medkit and batteries somewhere in the house, and every third wave the Wendigo comes hunting. It is slow, but every 20 seconds it dashes.
